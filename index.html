Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Moroccan students and parents need to know about international study procedures.

When should I start my Campus France / European application? ▼

The ideal time to start is between September and November of the academic year preceding your departure. For example, for a Fall 2026 intake, your file preparation, language certifications (TCF/DELF/IELTS), and dossier submission should begin as early as October 2025.

What are the financial guarantee requirements for a student visa? ▼

For France, the consulate requires an Attestation de Virement Irrévocable (AVI) of at least €615/month (€7,380/year) or an approved guarantor. For Germany, a blocked bank account (Sperrkonto) containing €11,208 is required. Our Kénitra team manages this entire process with certified banking partners.

Can I study in English in France, Germany, or Spain? ▼

Yes! Hundreds of Bachelor and Master programs in Germany, France, and Spain are taught 100% in English (especially in Business, Engineering, Computer Science, and Data Analytics). You will need an IELTS score (usually 6.0 - 6.5) or a recognized equivalent.

How does Afak Study Consulting guarantee student housing? ▼

We hold certified institutional partnerships with CROUS residences, private student residence networks, and vetted host families across France, Spain, and Germany. We provide you with the official Attestation d'hébergement required by consulates for your visa.

We welcome walk-in visits Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 18:30, though we strongly recommend booking a free session online or via WhatsApp to guarantee one-on-one time with an academic advisor.
